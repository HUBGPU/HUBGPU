How HubGPU works

Everything on HubGPU runs either on your own graphics card, in your browser, or on Robinhood Chain. This page says which is which.

What HubGPU is

An n-qubit quantum state is a list of 2n complex amplitudes. A GPU can hold that list in memory and apply gates to it exactly, so every graphics card is already a quantum simulator. HubGPU uses that in two ways:

  • Launchpad — tokens on Pons (Robinhood Chain) whose seal and birth record come from one measurement your GPU makes.
  • Quantum Lab — a circuit composer, a test of how many qubits your card can hold, and a leaderboard the server checks.

Compute pools and Proof-of-Qubit mining are on the roadmap and are not live.

How a token is born

  1. You name the token and pick a ticker.
  2. Your GPU (WebGPU) builds a random circuit of 12 qubits and 10 layers, seeded by the name, the ticker and a fresh random nonce, and computes the full statevector: 4,096 amplitudes.
  3. The state is measured once, using your browser's cryptographic randomness. The outcome is a 12-bit string such as |0110 1000 0100⟩. That is the token's birth record.
  4. The shape of the whole distribution draws the seal: 64 tiles, one per group of outcomes. The most likely groups are glazed black, the next grey, the rest white and raised. A ring marks where the measurement landed.

Not happy with the seal? Measure again runs a new circuit. Nothing is sent until you launch.

Rare births use 16 qubits (65,536 amplitudes): a 16×16 seal on a black plate. Only wallets holding enough $HGPU can launch one, and the contract enforces it.

Launching

One transaction from your own wallet launches the token on Pons and makes your first buy, which is required. Before you sign, the page simulates the whole launch; if it would fail, nothing is sent.

  • The seal is pinned to IPFS and becomes the token logo.
  • Born on HubGPU · |bits⟩ is written into the token's on-chain description.
  • The token's website is its own page here, hubgpu.network/t/<address>.
  • Creator fees and your optional creator tax are paid to your wallet.

A token appears on HubGPU only after the server reads the launch back from the chain and checks it.

Trading and creator fees

Every token trades on its Pons bonding curve until about 4.2 ETH has gone in; then it graduates to a Uniswap pool and trades on Pons. Each token page has a buy and sell box (8% slippage limit), a chart drawn from the curve's own trades, and the holder list.

Creators: My tokens lists everything your wallet launched and claims your creator fees in one go. Pons keeps them in an escrow until you claim.

Quantum Lab

The Composer places gates (H, X, Y, Z, S, T, rotations, CNOT, CZ, Swap) on up to 16 qubits. Every edit runs the statevector on your GPU in WebGPU compute shaders, one dispatch per gate, and shows outcome probabilities, 1,024 sampled shots, the largest amplitudes and each qubit's Bloch vector. A shorter Bloch arrow means the qubit is entangled.

Browsers without WebGPU fall back to an exact float64 simulator on the CPU, and the corner of the page says so.

Qubit Test and the board

The test grows a random circuit from 12 qubits up to the largest state your GPU will allocate and times every size. The ceiling is n = ⌊log₂(VRAM ÷ 8 bytes)⌋: an 80 GB card holds 33 qubits.

Before a result is listed, the server sends a seed for a 14-qubit check circuit. Your GPU's 16 most likely outcomes must match the server's own exact run within 0.0003. Correctness is verified; timing is measured in your browser and is labelled so.

FAQ

Is this a real quantum computer?

No. It is an exact classical simulation of one, on your GPU. For the sizes here (12 to 16 qubits for births, up to about 30 in the Qubit Test) a simulation gives the same answers a perfect quantum computer would, with no noise.

Can I choose my bitstring?

No. You can measure as many times as you like before launching, but each outcome comes from a fresh circuit and the browser's cryptographic randomness.

Which wallets work?

Any EVM browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet). The page switches it to Robinhood Chain.

Why does the lab say CPU mode?

Your browser has no WebGPU. Recent Chrome, Edge and Safari have it on most computers.

Where do my creator fees go?

To the wallet that launched the token. Claim them under My tokens.
