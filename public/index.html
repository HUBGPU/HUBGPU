01 / The idea

Quantum hardware is scarce. GPUs are everywhere.

Before a circuit ever touches a real QPU, it is written, debugged and verified on a classical machine that stores every amplitude. That machine is a GPU. The more memory it has, the more qubits it can hold, exactly, with no noise.

2n

Amplitudes per state

An n-qubit state is a list of 2n complex numbers. Thirty qubits is a billion of them.

8 B

Per amplitude

At complex64 precision each amplitude takes eight bytes, so memory, not speed, sets the ceiling.

+1

Qubit per doubling

Double the VRAM and the card holds one more qubit. Link cards and the ceiling rises again.

02 / The app · live

Quantum Lab runs on the GPU you already have.

No install and no account. Open the lab and your graphics card holds the quantum state through WebGPU, right in the browser.

A

Composer

Drop gates on up to 16 qubits and watch the state update on every click: outcome odds, sampled shots, amplitudes and each qubit's Bloch sphere.

B

Qubit Test

Grows a random circuit until your GPU runs out of room and times every size. One number comes out: how many qubits your card can hold.

C

Verified board

Before a score is listed, the server sends a 14-qubit check circuit and compares your GPU's answer with an exact run of its own.

D

Launchpad

Name a token and your GPU measures a 12-qubit circuit once. The outcome becomes its seal and its birth record, and it launches on Pons from your wallet.

Compute Pools

A launchpad for quantum research. Maintainers open funding rounds in ETH for their repo's circuits; GPU owners run the jobs and are paid by the second.

  • Rounds that fund in full or refund in full
  • Providers matched by how many qubits their card holds

Phase 3Later

Proof-of-Qubit Mining

Idle GPUs earn by simulating. The network hands out circuits, spot-checks the answers like the Qubit Test does, and pays miners from the $HGPU creator fees.

  • Mine from the browser, no install
  • Work is useful: real circuits, checked answers
  • Rewards from creator fees, paid on Robinhood Chain
